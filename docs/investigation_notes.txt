CLINIC BOOKING SUPPORT LAB
Missing Department Investigation
Date: 15 November 2026

PROJECT
A PostgreSQL learning project using 100 fictional patients,
200 referrals and 3 departments. No real patient data was used.

PROBLEM
Identify referrals with no department assigned.

METHOD
Used WHERE department_id IS NULL to find affected records.
Displayed referral_id, patient_id, referral_date,
department_id and status.
Sorted results by referral_id in ascending order.

QUERY
SELECT referral_id, patient_id, referral_date, department_id, status
FROM referrals
WHERE department_id IS NULL
ORDER BY referral_id ASC;

FINDINGS
11 referrals had a missing department ID.
Their referral IDs were:
123, 140, 157, 174, 191, 208, 225, 242, 259, 276, 293.

EXPORT CHECKS
Exported the query results and opened the file in Excel.
Confirmed 11 data rows, excluding the header, and 5 columns.
Confirmed referral IDs were sorted in ascending order.
Missing department values were displayed as NULL.
Widened the date column to display dates clearly.

NEXT ACTION
Ask the responsible team to confirm the correct department
against the source referral documentation before updating
each record. Do not guess the department.

STATUS
The 11 missing departments remain unresolved pending
confirmation. No changes were made to these records
during this investigation.

FILES
missing_department_check.sql - investigation query.
investigation_notes.txt - findings and next actions.
missing_department_referrals.csv - verified CSV results.


INVESTIGATION 2: OVERDUE PENDING REFERRALS

Reporting date: 12 November 2026

PROBLEM
Identify Pending referrals waiting more than 14 days.

METHOD
Joined referrals to patients to display patient names.
Used a LEFT JOIN to departments so referrals with missing
departments remained visible.
Calculated days_waiting by subtracting referral_date from
the reporting date.
Filtered for Pending status and waits greater than 14 days.
Sorted by days_waiting, longest first.

FINDINGS
30 referrals met the criteria.
Waiting times ranged from 16 to 28 days.
Two referrals had no department assigned. These were kept
visible so they could also be reviewed.
Pending status alone does not establish triage status or
clinical priority.

VALIDATION
Checked the export contained 30 data rows plus one header
row and four columns.
Confirmed waiting times were sorted from 28 down to 16 days.
Confirmed two department names were missing.

NEXT ACTION
Ask the responsible team to confirm the correct departments
against source documentation and review the pending referrals.
No records were changed during this investigation.

FILES
overdue_pending_referrals.sql - investigation query.
overdue_pending_referrals.csv - verified results export.

DATA
All records are fictional and used for learning purposes.
